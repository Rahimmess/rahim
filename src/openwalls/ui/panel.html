A wall is a layer stack, and nothing else. A partition is one layer; a cavity wall is five. Choose a framing standard on a structure or sheathing layer to generate studs, plates, opening frames and a cutting list instead of a solid slab. Types are saved inside this .skp — they travel with the file. Framing and header sizes are schematic and must be checked for the actual project.

Quantities are measured off the solids that were actually built, so an opening deduction cannot drift out of step with the model.
